rounded_headerbar_shape (src/lib.rs) is, as its author notes there,
heavily inspired by snui (https://gitlab.com/snakedye/snui) and so
technically licensed under the Mozilla Public License, version 2.0.
That function's source form is src/lib.rs of sctk-adwaita 0.10.1 on
crates.io (https://crates.io/crates/sctk-adwaita/0.10.1). The licence's
text is at https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/.
